YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy
Policy

Learn how VAAMIO collects, uses, stores and protects information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

Last updated: September 2026

VAAMIO respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect, why we use it, how we protect it and the choices available to you.

01

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with VAAMIO, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide or information generated when you use our website.

Information you provide

  • Name and contact details.
  • Delivery and billing information when placing an order.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Information submitted through contact, enquiry, bulk-order or business forms.
  • Information you provide when subscribing to newsletters or promotional communications.

Information collected automatically

  • Browser and device information.
  • General website usage information.
  • Pages viewed and interactions with the website.
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type and operating system where collected through website technologies.
02

How We Use Your Information

We may use information collected through the website for purposes such as:

  • Processing and fulfilling orders.
  • Communicating with you about your order or enquiry.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Improving our products, website and services.
  • Sending newsletters, offers and product updates where permitted and where you have chosen to receive them.
  • Detecting fraud, misuse or security issues.
  • Meeting applicable legal and business requirements.
03

Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers. Where applicable, your payment information may be handled directly by the relevant payment provider according to its own privacy and security practices.

VAAMIO does not need to store your complete card or payment credentials on its own website in order to process an online payment.

04

Cookies & Similar Technologies

VAAMIO may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage and improve your browsing experience.

Cookies may be used to remember preferences, support shopping functionality and help us understand how visitors interact with the website.

Your browser may allow you to control or restrict cookies. Some website functionality may not work properly if certain cookies are disabled.

05

Shopping Cart Information

VAAMIO may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to remember products added to your shopping cart and provide a smoother shopping experience.

This information is used to support the functionality of the website and does not by itself represent a complete customer profile.

06

Sharing Information

VAAMIO does not intend to sell your personal information as a standalone commercial product.

Information may be shared with appropriate service providers where necessary to operate the business, such as:

  • Delivery and logistics partners.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Website, hosting and technology providers.
  • Customer-support or communication providers.
  • Professional advisers or authorities where required by applicable law.
07

Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss or disclosure.

However, no website, transmission method or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08

Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes or meet applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted, anonymized or otherwise handled according to applicable requirements.

09

Your Choices

Depending on the nature of the information and the service involved, you may have choices regarding your personal information.

  • You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe option provided in the message.
  • You may contact us regarding information associated with your enquiries or orders.
  • You may manage certain browser cookie settings through your browser.
10

Children's Privacy

The VAAMIO website is intended for general audiences and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

11

Third-Party Websites

The VAAMIO website may contain links to third-party websites, social platforms, payment services or other external services.

Once you leave the VAAMIO website, the privacy practices of the third party will apply. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

12

Marketing Communications

Where applicable, VAAMIO may send information about new products, special offers, seasonal collections, recipes or other brand updates.

You can stop receiving promotional communications by using the unsubscribe mechanism included in the relevant communication or by contacting us.

13

Changes to This Privacy Policy

VAAMIO may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business, website, technologies or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

14

Contact & Privacy Enquiries

For questions, requests or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact VAAMIO using the details below.
